FAQ

Got questions? We've got answers

How long does the installation take?
Most tint installations are completed in 2–3 hours. We offer same-day service — wait in our lounge or we'll call you when your vehicle is ready. PPF installs typically take longer depending on coverage.
What does the lifetime warranty cover?
Peeling, bubbling, cracking, and color fade on our film, for as long as you own the vehicle. If it fails, we replace it — no charge.
Is the $99 price really for ceramic tint?
Yes. Every package uses premium ceramic film with 99% UV protection — we don't install dyed bargain film at any price point.
What shades are available?
You can choose 20%, 35%, or 50% on every package. Not sure which fits your car and local laws? We'll show you samples on your glass before we start.
Can I add the front windshield?
Yes — the Full Vehicle package includes the front windshield, and it can be added to any other package for an additional charge.
Do you handle commercial fleets?
That's our specialty. We offer volume pricing for 3+ vehicles, scheduled and after-hours service, and mobile installation at your yard so your fleet stays on the road.
What if I'm not satisfied with the installation?
Tell us within 7 days and we'll make it right — re-do the affected windows or refund the work. Your vehicle leaves looking the way you expected, period.
